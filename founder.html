Hello, I'm Ankit Prajapati, the founder of Vayra Media.

I started Vayra Media with a simple belief: every business deserves a strong digital presence and better opportunities to reach potential customers.

Today, people often search online before choosing a gym, clinic, restaurant, salon or service provider. But many good businesses still struggle to present themselves effectively online, connect with the right audience and turn interest into genuine inquiries.

That's the problem I want Vayra Media to help solve.

“

"A strong online presence is not just about being visible. It's about helping the right customers discover your business, trust your services and take the next step."

— ANKIT PRAJAPATI, FOUNDER

Our focus is to bring together website development, local SEO, digital marketing, branding and AI-powered automation to help businesses improve their online presence and connect more effectively with potential customers.

I believe digital solutions should do more than just look good. They should serve a clear purpose, solve real problems and support business growth.

At Vayra Media, every project starts with understanding the business, identifying opportunities and finding the right digital approach.

Our philosophy is simple: More Customers. More Growth.

We're building Vayra Media with a long-term vision: to become a trusted digital growth partner for businesses looking to move forward in an increasingly digital world.

Let's take your business forward, together.

Our Approach

Who We Help
